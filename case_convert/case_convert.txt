CASE CONVERT
============

PURPOSE
Scans text for all-lowercase or all-uppercase words and shows what they would look like in other cases (uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case).
Can also convert a specific word or phrase that you highlight and copy.

HOW TO USE
1. Copy the .cms and .py files to your My Paratext 9 Projects\cms folder.
2. To convert a specific word: highlight it in Paratext and press Ctrl+C, then run the check from Project menu > Tools > Custom Tools > Phil_new. The results will show all case variants for that word.
3. To scan a passage for all-lower or all-upper words: leave the FilterWord option empty and run the check normally.
4. Review the results list for conversion suggestions.

OPTIONS
  CheckLowercase
    Default:  Yes
    When Yes, flags words that are entirely lowercase and shows capitalized alternatives.

  CheckUppercase
    Default:  Yes
    When Yes, flags words that are entirely uppercase and shows lowercase alternatives.

  MinLetters
    Default:  2
    Minimum number of letters a word must contain before it is flagged.
    Single-letter words like "a" or "I" are skipped by default.

  FilterWord
    Default:  (empty)
    Enter a specific word or phrase here to see its case conversion options.
    If empty, the check scans all words in the passage. You can also populate this by highlighting text in Paratext and copying it to the clipboard before running the check.
