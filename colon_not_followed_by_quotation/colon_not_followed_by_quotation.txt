COLON NOT FOLLOWED BY QUOTATION
================================

PURPOSE
Finds places where a colon is followed by text that does not begin with a
quotation mark. This catches dialogue or quoted speech that may be missing
its opening quotation mark.

HOW TO USE
1. Copy the .cms and .py files to your My Paratext 9 Projects\cms folder.
2. In Paratext, run the check from Project menu > Tools > Custom Tools > Phil_new.
3. Review the results; each flagged item shows the colon and the text that
   follows it without a quotation mark.

OPTIONS
  QuotationMarks
    Default:  <<  "  --  '
    The characters that your translation uses to open a quotation. If the
    word after a colon starts with one of these characters it is not flagged.
    Update this to match your project's quotation style.

  CapitalizedOnly
    Default:  Yes
    When Yes, only flags cases where the first word after the colon begins
    with a capital letter. Set to No to flag all words regardless of case.
